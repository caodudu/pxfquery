# Goal Protocol

This goal handles journal and magazine research. Its outputs are evidence about journal ecology, published-paper patterns, accepted article forms, timing, affiliation signals, and publisher/journal policy context.

The current focus includes MDPI Genes, broader MDPI journals, and Frontiers in Genetics. Outputs should clarify:

- publication-cycle patterns and timing risk
- accepted article and deliverable forms
- bioinformatics/computational paper templates
- affiliation, country, and institution-type signals when relevant
- China + hospital + bioinformatics/computational published-presence patterns
- Peking Union Medical College Hospital / Beijing Union Medical College Hospital / PUMCH / 北京协和医院 related-unit published-presence patterns
- journal/publisher policy and external discussion context when relevant

This goal is separate from legacy asset migration. It may use previous journal-research tasks as inputs, but individual tasks should not be turned into project-specific PxFquery design unless their protocol explicitly says so. Some tasks are pure journal/publisher fact-finding tasks.
