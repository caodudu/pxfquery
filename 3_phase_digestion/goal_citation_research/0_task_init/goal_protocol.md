# Goal Protocol

This goal handles citation-focused research tasks.

Outputs should clarify:

- same-journal citation patterns
- within-publisher and cross-publisher citation patterns
- citation choices in accepted/published papers
- whether citation patterns correlate with observable outcomes such as publication timing, journal placement, article type, or paper family
- what can and cannot be inferred from published-paper data

This goal is separate from journal-affiliation research and from project-specific manuscript design. Published-paper data cannot directly estimate rejection rate, desk-reject rate, or true acceptance probability. Citation tasks must separate observable published-paper patterns from speculative editorial behavior.
